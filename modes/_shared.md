# India Job Market — Shared Context

## Salary Benchmarks (2024-2025, India)

| Level | Years Exp | Typical CTC Range |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| SDE-1 / Junior | 0-2 yrs | 6-15 LPA |
| SDE-2 / Mid | 2-5 yrs | 15-30 LPA |
| SDE-3 / Senior | 5-8 yrs | 30-55 LPA |
| Staff / Lead | 8+ yrs | 50-100+ LPA |

Product companies (Razorpay, CRED, Zepto, Swiggy, PhonePe, Groww) pay 20-40% above these ranges.
Service companies (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant) pay 20-30% below.

## Red Flags in Indian JDs
- "5+ years but freshers can apply" → confused requirements
- Salary "as per industry standards" with no range → likely underpaying
- "6 days/week mandatory" → work-life balance issue
- Pure C2H / contract-to-hire without conversion clarity → risky
- "Looking for immediate joiners only" with no flexibility → desperation hire

## Common JD Inflations to Discount
- "Worked on microservices" often means a monolith with one service split off
- "Led a team" sometimes means just being the most senior dev present
- "Millions of users" — ask what actual scale metrics look like

## Notice Period Norms
- 30 days: small startups, early-stage
- 60 days: standard for most product cos
- 90 days: large corps, banks, MNCs — hard to negotiate down
- "Immediate joiner" requests: almost always negotiable to 15-30 days

## Location Notes
- Bangalore (Bengaluru): highest concentration of tech cos, best salaries, high cost of living
- Hyderabad: growing fast, lower cost of living, many MNC offices
- Mumbai: fintech hub, higher cost of living
- Delhi NCR (Gurgaon/Noida): mix of product and services
- Pune: mix, lower cost than Bangalore
- Remote: still available but shrinking; product cos more open than services
